## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Hatsune Miku for Spotify in all eight looks
  - Eight color schemes generated from the palette: MikuDark, MikuLight, SakuraDark, SakuraLight, SnowDark, SnowLight, TetoDark and TetoLight.
  - One user.css for all of them: teal glow on play and progress, the look's second color on likes, shuffle and explicit badges, cards that lift on hover, a styled context menu, lyrics and queue.
  - Spotify's own surfaces stay in the palette instead of taking the tint of album art.
  - Install steps and fixes for Windows, Linux and Spotify updates in the README.
