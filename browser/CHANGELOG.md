## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Snow Miku and Kasane Teto on the new tab, frames and Zen
  - Eight looks to pick on the new tab; Snow Miku and Kasane Teto bring their second color from the art, strawberry pink and Teto's gold.
  - Chromium frame colors come in all eight looks.
  - The Zen mod lists every look, and each family can follow the system between dark and light.

### Changed

- Livelier second colors in Hatsune Miku and Sakura Miku, with the same backgrounds, text and accent.
